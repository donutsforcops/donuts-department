Everything sold on a donutsforcops stream is paid for, tracked and protected through Whatnot. We pack it like evidence and ship it fast. If something's wrong, you have a paper trail and a way to sort it out.

The Donuts Department (donutsforcops)

Policy

From the stream to your door

  1. You win it on the streamPayment goes through Whatnot with the card and address on your account. We never ask you to pay off the app.
  2. Same show, one packageEverything you win in the same show ships together, so you're not paying for a stack of separate boxes.
  3. Bagged and packedClothing is folded and bagged. Stickers ship flat and protected, and light sticker orders go out as letter mail.
  4. Shipped fastOrders go out quickly after the show. Tracking shows up in your Whatnot app as soon as the label is scanned.
  5. DeliveredCheck your order in the Whatnot app. If anything is off, message donutsforcops there first. It's the fastest way to fix it.

Authenticity

  • We only run authentic Supreme, Strangelove and FTP. No reps, no "inspired by."
  • Condition, size and season are called out on stream. If you're unsure about something, ask in chat before you bid. That's what it's for.
  • donutsforcops is an independent reseller, not affiliated with Supreme, Strangelove or FTP.

If something goes wrong

  • Message donutsforcops in the Whatnot app with your order and a photo of the issue.
  • Every order is covered by Whatnot's own buyer protection, so you're never relying on a handshake.
